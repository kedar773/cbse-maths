## 💡 About The Website

**Kedar's Academy Mathematics Engine** is an interactive, web-based digital learning portal designed specifically for CBSE Class 11 and Class 12 students.

Traditional digital textbooks and PDF notes can feel rigid, intimidating, and fatiguing to read. This website recreates the natural, warm, and intuitive atmosphere of a **teacher writing directly on a classroom whiteboard**:

* **Whiteboard Aesthetic**: Natural handwriting fonts, vibrant marker highlights, hand-drawn sticky note callouts, and clean porcelain boards.
* **Strict NCERT Alignment**: The core text contains zero extra or out-of-syllabus fluff. Every explanation, definition, and theorem adheres directly to the prescribed CBSE syllabus.
* **Safe Competitive Segregation**: Material that extends beyond the board examination syllabus (such as JEE Main & Advanced shortcuts, rationalized proofs, and competitive problem patterns) is strictly isolated into a separate "Advanced Studies" section. Students preparing solely for the board exams can study with complete confidence knowing every word in the core section is 100% relevant to their board paper.
* **Step-by-Step Scoring Transparency**: Model answers feature explicit CBSE marking breakdowns, teaching students not just the final answer, but the exact step discipline required to secure full marks.

## 📚 The 5-Tab Learning Architecture

Every single chapter in the curriculum is organized into five structured study tabs:

### 1. 📋 NCERT Core Notes
* Lucid, conversational explanations breaking down abstract mathematical concepts into simple, intuitive ideas.
* Essential definitions, geometric interpretations, and algebraic properties.
* Color-coded sticky notes highlighting critical identities, domain/range constraints, and fundamental theorems.

### 2. 💡 Solved Examples & CBSE Step Marking
* Carefully graded foundational and textbook examples worked out with official CBSE step-marking breakdowns (`[½ Mark]`, `[1 Mark]`, `[1.5 Marks]`).
* Demonstrates how board examiners evaluate steps, including statement of formulas, substitution, intermediate algebraic simplifications, and concluding statements with correct units.

### 3. 🎯 Board Exam PYQ Vault (2020–2025)
* A comprehensive repository of **372 authentic CBSE board exam questions** from recent years (2020 through 2025).
* Categorized by official question typologies:
  * **1-Mark MCQs & Assertion-Reasoning**: Speed-tested conceptual questions.
  * **2-Mark Very Short Answer (VSA)**: Two-step evaluations.
  * **3-Mark Short Answer (SA)**: Multi-step standard problems.
  * **4-Mark Case-Based Questions**: Real-world context passages with sub-questions.
  * **5-Mark Long Answer (LA)**: Comprehensive derivations, proofs, and multi-part questions.
* Every single question includes a complete, self-contained model solution with mark allocations for every step.

### 4. ⚠️ Examiner Traps & Speed Hacks
* **Common Student Blunders**: Highlights subtle traps where students routinely lose marks (e.g., dividing by zero variables, forgetting $\pm$ when taking square roots, omitting the constant of integration $+C$, or ignoring domain restrictions).
* **Speed Hacks & Verification Tricks**: Practical shortcut formulas, symmetry checks, and quick dimensional checks to verify solutions before submitting the exam paper.

### 5. 🚀 Advanced Studies & Competitive Extension
* Specially reserved for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and entrance exam aspirants.
* Includes deeper derivations, rationalized NCERT topics (such as elementary row operations for matrix inverse, Rolle's and Mean Value Theorems, scalar triple products), and competitive problem-solving patterns.
* Completely segregated so core CBSE candidates never feel confused or overloaded.

## ✏️ Interactive Classroom Whiteboard Features

* **Whiteboard Scribble & Scratchpad Drawer**: A built-in virtual canvas accessible from anywhere on the website. Students can select pens, highlighters, colored markers, and an eraser to scribble equations, test derivations, or solve rough calculations without leaving the webpage.
* **Global Topic & Formula Search**: Quickly locate any definition, theorem, formula, or board question across all 27 chapters with a quick keyword search (`Ctrl+K`).
* **Interactive Whiteboard Grid Switcher**: Toggle the whiteboard background between clean porcelain, dotted grid, or engineering grid pattern depending on personal study preference.
